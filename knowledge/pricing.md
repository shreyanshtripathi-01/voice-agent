# Pricing knowledge

## Starter plan
The Starter plan is priced at ₹1,50,000 per year. It includes one AI voice agent, up to 5,000 minutes of calls per year, English and Hindi, basic CRM write-back, and email support.

## Growth plan
The Growth plan is ₹3,50,000 per year. It includes three agent personas, 25,000 minutes, English, Hindi and Spanish, RAG over private documents, appointment booking, sentiment analytics, and priority support.

## Enterprise plan
Enterprise is custom priced, starting at ₹9,00,000 per year. It adds on-premise or private-cloud deployment, custom integrations, a dedicated success manager, and a service-level agreement.

## Commercial note
Pilot projects can start on the Starter plan and upgrade after the first quarter. There is no setup fee in the pilot programme, and the demo you are seeing uses only free open tools.
